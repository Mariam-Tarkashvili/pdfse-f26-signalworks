## Step 2: The hypothesis we test first
> **We believe** second-year KIU students with short breaks between classes, like Ana,
> **struggle with** not knowing how long the cafeteria queue will take before they commit to waiting
> **when** they reach the cafeteria door mid-day with only 20–40 minutes until the next class (especially exam weeks),
> **which costs them** skipping the meal, arriving ~15 minutes late to class, or spending about 5 GEL on shop snacks instead.
> **Today they** peek at the line, ask a friend already waiting, skip lunch, or leave for a shop.
> **We are wrong if** students describing the last time they skipped lunch or arrived late do not mention uncertainty about wait time (guessing at the door / no way to know) before we bring it up.

## Step 3: The beliefs we did not pick
Each one becomes at least one question in your interview script, so you still hear about it.
- Short break / next class pressure (Beka) → script question 6: "Walk me through the last time your break was too short to eat. What did you do?"
- Peak rush when classes end together (Luka) → script question 7: "Tell me about the last exam-week lunch. What was different from a normal day?"
