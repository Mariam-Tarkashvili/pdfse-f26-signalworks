## The strongest incumbent

- Looking at the line from the door. It appears in more logs than any other check (01, 04, 10, and 05 describes its result), it costs nothing and takes seconds, so every student does it before anything else; the complaint is only that it shows the crowd, not the time: "the line looks small from the outside, but inside it gets stuck at the register" (05).

## Alternatives nobody mentioned

- A wait-time or queue-tracking service of any kind. One interviewee asked to be told if a "real-time queue tracker" is launched (06), but nobody uses one, so it is not a row.
- Pre-ordering or paying ahead at the canteen counter.
- A notice or schedule from the canteen about its busy times.
