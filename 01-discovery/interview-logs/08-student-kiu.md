# Interview 08 · Student, 2nd year, management · (name: Nata)

**Week:** 2 · **Asker:** MT · **Logger:** BR · **Consent:** yes; notes only, no recording
**Real or practice:** Real

**Context:** Second-year management student. On Mondays and Wednesdays her only break is 14:00 to 14:40, later than most groups, and the lecture after it is with a lecturer who grades attendance. Not in this course.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if students describing the last time they skipped lunch or arrived late do not mention uncertainty about wait time (guessing at the door / no way to know) before we bring it up.

**Quotes** (Georgian as spoken; English beside it; ★ = surprise)
- "ოცი წუთი ვიდექი და ჩემამდე რომ მოვიდა რიგი, საჭმელი აღარ იყო." / "I stood for twenty minutes, and when my turn came there was food left." ★ (minute 5, answering question 3)
- "ლექციაზე შვიდი წუთით დამაგვიანდა და ლექტორმა დასწრების ფურცელზე ხელი არ მომაწერინა." / "I was seven minutes late to the lecture and the lecturer did not let me sign the attendance sheet."
- "რომ მცოდნოდა, რომ საჭმელი თავდებოდა, საერთოდ არ ჩავდგებოდი." / "If I had known the food was running out, I would not have joined the line at all."

**Workaround:** None that works. She sometimes asks people walking out with trays what is left, and twice she went to the shop first and skipped the canteen completely.
**Cost:** One attendance mark lost in a course where attendance counts toward the grade. Twenty minutes of a forty-minute break, then 4 GEL on a khachapuri from the shop, eaten after the lecture.
**Frequency:** The food ran out before her turn twice in the last three weeks, both times after 14:00. Late to the lecture once.
**Prediction check:** Partly. She raised "not knowing" herself, before we mentioned it, but what she could not know was whether any food would be left, not how long the wait would be.
**Surprise ★:** There are two unknowns at the door, not one: how long the line takes and what is still there at the end of it. For students with a late break, the second one is worse.
**Commitment:** Introduced a groupmate with the same Monday break; agreed we can come back when we have something to show.
**Next:** Candidate question for script v2: "Tell me about the last time you got to the front and could not get what you came for."
