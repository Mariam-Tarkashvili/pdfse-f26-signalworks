# Interview 07 · Student, 2nd year, computer science · (name: Tina)


**Week:** 2 · **Asker:** MT · **Logger:** BR · **Consent:** yes; notes only, no recording
**Real or practice:** Real

**Context:** Second-year computer science student who lives off campus. Tuesdays and Thursdays she has three lectures with one 30-minute break at 13:00, and she eats at the canteen about three times a week. Not in this course.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if students describing the last time they skipped lunch or arrived late do not mention uncertainty about wait time (guessing at the door / no way to know) before we bring it up.

**Quotes** (Georgian as spoken; English beside it; ★ = surprise)
- "კართან ვდგავარ და ვერ ვხვდები, ხუთი წუთია თუ ოცდახუთი." / "I stand at the door and can't tell whether it's five minutes or twenty-five." (minute 6, answering question 2, before we said anything about waiting)
- "სამშაბათს ჩავდექი, თხუთმეტი წუთი ვიცადე და მაინც წამოვედი, ლექცია მეწყებოდა." / "On Tuesday I joined, waited fifteen minutes and left anyway, my lecture was starting." ★
- "ჯგუფელს ვწერ: რიგი როგორია? ხან მპასუხობს, ხან არა." / "I text a groupmate: how's the line? Sometimes she answers, sometimes not." (she showed the chat: four messages like this in two weeks)

**Workaround:** Texts a groupmate whose break starts earlier. If there is no answer, she buys something at the shop outside the gate.
**Cost:** Skipped lunch twice last week. About 6 GEL each time at the shop, and on Tuesday she did not eat until 17:00.
**Frequency:** Every Tuesday and Thursday, the two days with the 30-minute break.
**Prediction check:** Yes. She described guessing at the door herself, in answer to question 2, before we mentioned waiting.
**Surprise ★:** A long line does not bother her when she knows it is long. What hurts is spending fifteen minutes of a thirty-minute break and leaving with nothing.
**Commitment:** Named two groupmates with the same timetable; agreed we can come back when we have something to show.
**Next:** Ask the groupmates about the last time they left a line halfway through.
