# Interview 03 - 1st-year student, on campus - (code name: MGT-Y1)

**Week:** 3 - **Asker:** BR - **Logger:** MT

**Consent:** yes; notes only, no recording

**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** Freshman living on campus, relies on cafeteria food most days. Falls back to dorm cooking or delivery when the line fails them.

**Our prediction:** We are wrong if students describing the last time they skipped lunch or arrived late do not mention uncertainty about wait time (guessing at the door / no way to know) before we bring it up.

**Quotes** (Georgian as spoken; English beside it)
- "ერთხელ 20 წუთი ვიდექი და ბოლოს საჭმელი საერთოდ გათავდა." / "Once I stood for 20 minutes and in the end they ran out of food entirely."
- "თუ რიგი დიდია, უბრალოდ ოთახში ავდივარ და საჭმელს ვიძახებ." / "If the line is big, I just go up to my room and order food delivery."
- "არასდროს ვიცი როდის არის ბევრი ხალხი, წინასწარ ვერ განსაზღვრავ, ყოველ დღე სხვადასხვა დროსაა." / "I never know when there are many people, you can't determine it in advance, it's at different times every day."

**Workaround:** Walks into the cafeteria; if the line is too long, returns to dorm for delivery or instant noodles.

**Cost:** 8-10 GEL extra on delivery; once wasted ~20 minutes standing and still got no food.

**Frequency:** 2-3 times a week.

**Prediction check:** Yes. Raised unpredictability of busy times immediately when describing what happens.

**What surprised us:** Beyond wait time, running out of food after standing in line was a primary frustration - time lost and no meal.

**Commitment:** Introduced roommate who often orders delivery with them when the line is bad.

**Next:** Interview roommate (MGT-Y1-B) about shared delivery costs after long cafeteria waits.

Written within 24 hours.
