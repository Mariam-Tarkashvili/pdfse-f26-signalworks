# Interview 10 · Student, 3rd year, canteen regular · (name: Nikoloz)


**Week:** 2 · **Asker:** LM · **Logger:** MT · **Consent:** yes; notes only, no recording
**Real or practice:** Real

**Context:** Third-year student who eats at the canteen four or five days a week and has done so for two years. His breaks are 40 minutes or longer. Not in this course.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if students describing the last time they skipped lunch or arrived late do not mention uncertainty about wait time (guessing at the door / no way to know) before we bring it up.

**Quotes** (Georgian as spoken; English beside it; ★ = surprise)
- "ჩვეულებრივ დღეს ვიცი: პირველ საათზე სავსეა, ორის ნახევარზე უკვე ცარიელია." / "On a normal day I know: at one o'clock it is full, by half past one it is already empty."
- "გამოცდების კვირაში ეს აღარ მუშაობს. ყველა ერთად გამოდის და ვერაფერს გათვლი." / "In exam week that stops working. Everyone comes out at once and you cannot predict anything." ★ (minute 8, answering question 3, before we asked about exam weeks)
- "შორიდან ვიყურები. ხალხი თუ კარამდე დგას, აღარც მივდივარ." / "I look from a distance. If people are standing all the way to the door, I don't even go in."

**Workaround:** On normal days he waits out the first twenty minutes of the break and goes when the rush has passed. He checks from a window upstairs before walking down.
**Cost:** In the last midterm week he skipped lunch on three days out of five and spent about 5 GEL a day on snacks instead. In normal weeks, close to nothing.
**Frequency:** Normal weeks: about once every two weeks. Exam weeks: most days.
**Prediction check:** Yes, but only for exam weeks. His last skipped lunch was in midterm week, and he said himself that he could not predict the line. On normal days he says he already knows.
**Surprise ★:** A regular has solved the problem for normal days by learning the pattern. It comes back only when the pattern breaks, which fits Luka's belief about peak rush as much as it fits ours.
**Commitment:** Offered to introduce two first-years from his dorm floor who "still get it wrong"; agreed we can come back when we have something to show.
**Next:** Interview the first-years. Check whether students who have not learned the pattern yet have the problem every week.
