# Interview 09 · Master's student, lives off campus · (name: Anano)

**Week:** 2 · **Asker:** BR · **Logger:** LM · **Consent:** yes; notes only, no recording
**Real or practice:** Real

**Context:** First-year master's student who commutes about 50 minutes each way. Her classes are packed into three days, with 15-minute breaks between them. She has eaten at the canteen once this semester. Not in this course.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if students describing the last time they skipped lunch or arrived late do not mention uncertainty about wait time (guessing at the door / no way to know) before we bring it up.

**Quotes** (Georgian as spoken; English beside it; ★ = surprise)
- "თხუთმეტი წუთი მაქვს. სასადილოზე არც ვფიქრობ." / "I have fifteen minutes. I don't even think about the canteen."
- "სახლიდან მომაქვს კონტეინერით და დერეფანში ვჭამ." / "I bring a container from home and eat in the corridor." (she showed it: a lunchbox and a fork in her bag)
- "რიგი საერთოდ რომ არ იყოს, მაინც ვერ მოვასწრებდი დაჯდომას და ჭამას." / "Even if there were no line at all, I still would not have time to sit down and eat." ★

**Workaround:** Cooks the evening before and carries a lunchbox. Eats standing in the corridor outside the next lecture room.
**Cost:** About 20 minutes of cooking the night before each class day. Last week she forgot the lunchbox once and did not eat from 9:00 to 16:00.
**Frequency:** All three class days, every week.
**Prediction check:** No. She never mentioned not knowing the wait. The length of her break decides for her before she gets anywhere near the canteen door.
**Surprise ★:** She ruled the canteen out at the start of the semester, from her timetable alone. For her there is no moment of guessing at the door, which is the moment our hypothesis is built on.
**Commitment:** Named one coursemate with the same timetable; said we can come back, but warned she is only on campus three days a week.
**Next:** Supports Beka's belief. Record the break length for every interview and check whether the problem only appears for breaks of 30 minutes or more.
