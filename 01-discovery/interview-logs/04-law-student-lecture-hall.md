# Interview 04 · student, 2nd-year law, KIU · (code name KIU-1)

**Week:** 3 · **Asker:** LM · **Logger:** BR · **Consent:** yes; notes only, no recording · **Real or practice:** real

**Context:** 2nd-year Law student who attends back-to-back lectures and frequently tries to grab lunch during the 20-minute afternoon break. Interviewed Sat 10 Oct 17:00.
**Our prediction:** We are wrong if students describing the last time they skipped lunch or arrived late do not mention uncertainty about wait time (guessing at the door / no way to know) before we bring it up.

**Workaround:** Walks up to the canteen glass door during the break; if there are more than 5-6 people queued, walks away immediately without entering and buys a snickers or coffee from the hallway vending machine.
**Cost:** 2 skipped lunches per week; poor concentration during 15:00 seminar.
**Frequency:** 2–3 times a week during peak break hours (13:00–14:00).
**Prediction check:** **yes** — Student unprompted mentioned "no way of knowing if it'll take 5 or 20 minutes" within the first 2 minutes of describing their last skipped meal.
**Surprise ★:** The student decides to abandon lunch *before* even stepping inside the line, purely based on the inability to estimate queue speed from the doorway.
**Commitment:** **follow-up** — Agreed to send a photo/message next time they check the doorway and decide to turn around.
