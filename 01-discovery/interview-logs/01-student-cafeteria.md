# Interview 01 - 2nd-year student, short break - (code name: CS-Y2)

**Week:** 3 - **Asker:** BR - **Logger:** MT

**Consent:** yes; notes only, no recording

**Real or practice:** real

**Context:** 2nd-year CS student at KIU with a 20-minute break between lectures. Relies on the cafeteria when the break allows; otherwise falls back to campus shop/Spar.

**Our prediction:** We are wrong if students describing the last time they skipped lunch or arrived late do not mention uncertainty about wait time (guessing at the door / no way to know) before we bring it up.

**Quotes** (Georgian as spoken; English beside it)
- "რიგს რომ ვხედავ, საერთოდ აღარ შევდივარ, სენდვიჩს ვყიდულობ სპარში." / "When I see the line, I don't even enter, I just buy a sandwich at Spar."
- "ორჯერ ლექციაზე დავაგვიანე 15 წუთით, იმიტომ რომ რიგი ნელა მოძრაობდა." / "Twice I was 15 minutes late to class because the line was moving so slowly."
- "წინასწარ არასდროს იცი 5 წუთი დაგჭირდება თუ 25." / "You never know in advance whether you'll need 5 minutes or 25."

**Workaround:** Peeks at the line from the entrance; if it extends past the doors, walks straight to Spar/campus shop for snacks instead of entering.

**Cost:** 6 GEL on snacks; 15 minutes late to lecture (twice mentioned).

**Frequency:** 3-4 times a week in regular weeks; every day in exam weeks.

**Prediction check:** Yes. Raised unpredictable wait times and long queues while describing the routine, before we named wait uncertainty.

**What surprised us:** If the line extends past the doorway, they abandon hot cafeteria meals entirely and switch to shop snacks - the doorway is their go/no-go signal.

**Commitment:** Introduction to a classmate who frequently skips lunch during exams.

**Next:** Interview that classmate about exam-week lunch habits. Ask how they decide at the door.

Written within 24 hours.
