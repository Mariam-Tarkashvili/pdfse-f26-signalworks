## Pattern 1

**Pattern:** Students cannot tell how long the cafeteria wait will take before they commit to the line.

**Interviews behind it:** 01, 02, 03

**Quotes:**
- "You never know in advance whether you'll need 5 minutes or 25." (01)
- "I usually text a friend - 'Is the line big?' - and then go down." (02)
- "I never know when there are many people, you can't determine it in advance, it's at different times every day." (03)

## Pattern 2

**Pattern:** When the line looks too long or too risky, students leave the cafeteria for Spar, delivery, or skip lunch entirely.

**Interviews behind it:** 01, 02, 03

**Quotes:**
- "When I see the line, I don't even enter, I just buy a sandwich at Spar." (01)
- "During exam week I don't eat lunch at all, I sit hungry until evening." (02)
- "I'd rather take a quiz hungry than stand in that queue." (02)
- "If the line is big, I just go up to my room and order food delivery." (03)

## Pattern 3

**Pattern:** Standing in the queue costs class time or wasted minutes that still end with no meal.

**Interviews behind it:** 01, 03

**Quotes:**
- "Twice I was 15 minutes late to class because the line was moving so slowly." (01)
- "Once I stood for 20 minutes and in the end they ran out of food entirely." (03)
