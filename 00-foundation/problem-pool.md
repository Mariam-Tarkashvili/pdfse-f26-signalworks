AUTHOR: Mariam Tarkashvili
WHO I watched: KIU students, including me, who take the bus number 3 or 25 from home or work to campus for 9:00 classes, or vice versa
STRUGGLE I saw: on weekday mornings, we wait 20–60 minutes with no way to know if the next bus is stuck in traffic, waiting for the train at the station, or has skipped a round because it bunched up with the bus ahead; Moovit shows a schedule that doesn't match what arrives
COST I can name: arriving late to classes, 6-15 GEL on a taxi after giving up, up to an hour lost per trip

AUTHOR: Mariam Tarkashvili
WHO I watched: me and other students new to Kutaisi shopping for produce at the Kutaisi central market
STRUGGLE I saw: every weekend, walking between stalls where nothing has a price tag, with no way to tell which vendor has good quality or whether the price quoted is fair, so ending up accepting the first number given
COST I can name: paying 20% more than my friend paid for the same item, throwing out potatoes that spoiled within 5 days, 30+ minutes wandering stall to stall

AUTHOR: Mariam Tarkashvili
WHO I watched: KIU students with a short break between classes or during exams
STRUGGLE I saw: At unpredictable times during weekdays, especially, during exam weeks, the cafeteria line is so long that students either skip lunch or arrive late to their next class or exam
COST I can name: 10 students leaving the queue without eating, arriving 15 minutes late to the next class, 5 GEL spent on shop snacks instead of a meal
