# User Observation Notes

## 1. KIU Bus Reliability

**AUTHOR:** Mariam Tarkashvili

**WHO I watched:**  
KIU students, including me, who take bus **#3 or #25** from home or work to campus for 9:00 classes, or vice versa.

**STRUGGLE I saw:**  
On weekday mornings, we wait **20–60 minutes** with no way to know if the next bus is stuck in traffic, waiting for the train at the station, or has skipped a round because it bunched up with the bus ahead. Moovit shows a schedule that doesn't match what actually arrives.

**COST I can name:**
- Arriving late to classes
- **6–15 GEL** spent on a taxi after giving up
- Up to **1 hour lost per trip**

## 2. Kutaisi Central Market Shopping

**AUTHOR:** Mariam Tarkashvili

**WHO I watched:**  
Me and other students new to Kutaisi shopping for produce at the **Kutaisi Central Market**.

**STRUGGLE I saw:**  
Every weekend, we walk between stalls where nothing has a price tag, with no way to tell which vendor has good quality or whether the quoted price is fair. As a result, we often end up accepting the first number given.

**COST I can name:**
- Paying **20% more** than a friend paid for the same item
- Throwing out potatoes that spoiled within **5 days**
- **30+ minutes** spent wandering from stall to stall

## 3. KIU Cafeteria Queues

**AUTHOR:** Mariam Tarkashvili

**WHO I watched:**  
KIU students with a short break between classes or during exams.

**STRUGGLE I saw:**  
At unpredictable times during weekdays, especially during exam weeks, the cafeteria line becomes so long that students either skip lunch or arrive late to their next class or exam.

**COST I can name:**
- **10 students** leaving the queue without eating
- Arriving **15 minutes late** to the next class
- **5 GEL** spent on shop snacks instead of a proper meal
