# Ideal Customer Profile

**Name (invented) and one-line sketch:** Ana, 20, second-year KIU student with back-to-back classes and a short break that is her only chance to eat on campus.

**Their situation:** She lives near campus or in a student flat in Kutaisi, carries a full weekday timetable, and eats at the **KIU cafeteria** when she has time between lectures. On busy days she checks the line with her eyes from the door, sometimes asks a friend already in the queue how long it looks, and otherwise has no signal for whether she can finish a meal before the next class.

**The moment the problem shows up:** Weekday mid-day, especially **exam weeks**, when her break is only **20–40 minutes** and the cafeteria line is already out the door. She has to decide in that moment: wait and risk being late, skip lunch, or leave and buy something elsewhere.

**What it costs them:** Leaving the queue without eating (Mariam saw about **10 students** do this in one rush). Arriving **~15 minutes late** to the next class or exam. Spending about **5 GEL** on shop snacks instead of a proper meal.

**What they do about it today:** Peek at the line and guess; ask a friend already waiting; skip lunch entirely; or leave and buy chips / a pastry from a nearby shop and eat while walking to class.

**What they are NOT:**
- Students who always bring lunch from home and never use the cafeteria: the queue is not their problem.
- Staff / visitors who can eat whenever they want with no next lecture: they can wait without the same cost.
