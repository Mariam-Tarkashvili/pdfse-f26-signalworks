**One moment I broke the Mom Test, and what I asked instead next time** (name the log):

In log 07, after she described texting her groupmate, I asked "Would it help if you could see how long the line is before you go down?" She said "yes, of course", which told us nothing, because anyone says yes to a hypothetical. In log 08 I asked "Tell me about the last time you got to class late after a break" instead, and got the real story: twenty minutes in the line, no hot food left, and the attendance mark refused.
